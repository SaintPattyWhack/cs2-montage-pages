Personal software

cs2-montage

A single-user tool that turns my own Counter-Strike 2 gameplay recordings into short vertical highlight videos, and uploads them to my own YouTube channel.

What it does

It watches the game's own telemetry feed while I play, tells OBS when to record, cuts clips around the moments I got a kill, assembles them into a short montage with licensed music, and stages the result for me to review. Videos I approve are uploaded to my channel.

Who can use it

Only me. It runs on one desktop computer, is not distributed, has no sign-up, no server, and no other users. It is not a product and is not offered to anyone else.

Why it asks for Google access

  • youtube.upload — to upload finished videos to my channel.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read how those videos performed, so the tool can tell which editing choices worked.
  • drive.file — to store finished videos in my own Drive instead of filling the local disk. This is the narrow Drive scope: it can only touch files the application itself created.

Data

It handles my gameplay footage and my own channel's statistics, entirely on my computer and in my own Google account. Nothing is sent to any third party. See the privacy policy.
